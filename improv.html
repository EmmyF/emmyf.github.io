Improvisation

I believe improv is an essential and transformative art, and I feel so lucky to have had the opprotunity to perform as part of Rollins Improv Players for the past three years. Improv gives us the ability to tell the stories we feel passionate about, and to create something along with our audience. Ultimately, theatre is about community, and improv lets us bring the audience into our community.
