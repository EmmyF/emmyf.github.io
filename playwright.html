Playwrighting

I've had the incredible opprotunity to see two plays I wrote be produced. Space Play was performed in a staged reading produced by Rollins Players in 2024, and The Mirror's Other Side was taken on by a wonderful group of students in Spirit Elementary's Drama Club in 2020.

Currently, I'm co-writing Four Dinners, which is set to be part of the 2026 Orlando International Fringe Theatre Festival.

Four Dinners is a new play about sisters, Penny and Charlie, navigating their 20s as queer women from a sheltered background. Joined by roommate, Ynez, and girlfriend, Talia, the play takes place across four different dinners in Penny’s apartment. The play delves into desire, belonging, and girlhood. As a play about four queer women written by two queer women, it was accepted into the Orlando Fringe Festival under the Amplified Voices Lottery.
