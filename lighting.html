Lighting Design

I fell in love with lighting design when I was 19, and I've been doing it at every chance since. I adore the opprotunity to work with the rest of a production's team to immerse the audience in the mood and world of a show. I'm always in search of new ways to think about lighting design and new techniques to give a show as much life and magic as possible.
I'm currently lighting designing Four Dinners, part of the Orlando Fringe Fesitval happening in May. Learn more about that production here!
